Photographs are not covered by the repository's code license. The packaged JPEGs are unmodified downloads; display/export may crop, resize and overlay text. Credits remain visible in both wallpaper modes. Official source pages and usage links are also shown in the reading view.

NASA media guidelines are a usage policy, not a blanket CC license. No NASA/ESA/ESO logos, agency endorsement or exclusive rights are claimed. Check the linked terms when reusing material separately.

Editorial notes are independently written summaries with per-section source URLs. Infrared color mapping, visible-light mosaics and ground-based photographs are described separately. The packaged catalog records the exact SHA-256 and pixel dimensions of each image.
